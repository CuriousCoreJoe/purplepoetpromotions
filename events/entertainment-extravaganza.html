Entertainment Extravaganza
Three Times A Charm

Spoken word, song, comedy, and live music — an unforgettable evening presented by Purple Poet Promotions.

Saturday, March 21 4:00 PM – 7:00 PM (Doors at 3:45 PM) MSU Management Education Center, Troy, MI 186 Attendees

About This Event

The sounds of The Rhythm Method of Detroit, spoken word and song of One Single Rose, Purple Poet, and the comedy of Angela Verges and Amy Huber-Smith came together for an extraordinary evening of entertainment at the MSU Management Education Center in Troy, MI.

Plenty of free parking. Ages 18 and over. Three hours of live performances across music, spoken word, comedy, and song.

The Lineup

The Rhythm Method of Detroit

Featuring the saxophone of Amy Decker and lead and bass guitars of D.J. Johnson and Derrick Thomas, accompanied by a powerhouse of musicians and vocalists. The Rhythm Method of Detroit has shared the stage with the likes of Earth, Wind and Fire, Ronnie Laws, and Ollie Woodson of the Temptations.

One Single Rose®

International spoken word artist, playwright, author, and songstress. Expect to be blown away.

Angela Verges & Amy Huber-Smith

Comedians who have performed at venues such as Ridleys Comedy Castle, One Night Stanz, and Strange Brew. They performed at Ridleys Comedy Castle's "Age Is Just a Number" on April 1st — no fooling!

Purple Poet

Local spoken word artist and host. Guaranteed to make you feel the words.
