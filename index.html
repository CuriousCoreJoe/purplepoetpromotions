Our Story

About Purple Poet Promotions

Purple Poet, a published poet herself, has been a poet since she was a child and loves purple.

She has an appreciation for all types of creativity and decided that it was time to use her time and skills to spread joy to others by bringing beautiful noise and people together in beautiful spaces for entertainment.

Follow Us on Facebook

Our Goal

The goal of this site is to showcase past and present events hosted by Purple Poet Promotions and to afford subscribers and viewers an opportunity to stay informed about upcoming events and ticketing opportunities.

There will also be opportunities for performing artists to submit interest requests, performance opportunities, and for prospective clients in search of talent to submit direct inquiries to Purple Poet Promotions (P3).

Whether it be a corporate event, a private event such as a birthday or wedding, or just because, there is a pathway to fulfilling any need for many types of entertainment. This will be the "one stop" pathway to entertainment.
